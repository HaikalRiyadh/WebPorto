# AntiSlop UI Audit Report

- **Target**: Portfolio Website (`index.html`, `style.css`)
- **Audit ID**: audit-001
- **Date**: 2026-10-26
- **Mode**: AFTER (Audit & Prioritized Findings)
- **Standard**: AntiSlop Ruleset R-01 to R-38 (Craftsmanship Standard C-1 to C-5)

---

## Executive Summary

The audited portfolio contains strong authentic content (real software projects, verified credentials at PT Telkom Infra, and genuine technical stacks). However, the interface currently suffers from severe **Trend-Stacking** and **AI Slop Patterns** (unnecessary floating orbs, particle canvas backgrounds, cursor spotlight followers, universal glassmorphism, and multiple em dash copywriting violations).

The findings below are prioritized strictly by rule tiers:
1. **HIGH Priority (Hard Gate)**: Copywriting integrity, accessibility, tap targets, and contrast.
2. **MEDIUM Priority (Purpose-Gate)**: Unnecessary decorative techniques without stated architectural purpose (canvas particles, ambient orbs, excessive glassmorphism, glow saturation).
3. **LOW Priority (Quality Locks)**: Typographic consistency, buzzword elimination, rhythm, and palette discipline.

---

## Prioritized Findings List

### Tier 1: HIGH Priority (Hard Gate — Absolute Failures)

1. **[R-02 Copywriting — Em Dash Violation]**
   - **Location**: `index.html` lines 7, 15, 17, 953, 968, 982, 996, 1011, 1025.
   - **Issue**: Em dash character (`—`) is used in page titles, meta tags, and timeline date ranges (e.g. `Haikal Riyadh Romadhon — Web & Mobile Developer`, `Aug 2026 — Present`).
   - **Required Fix**: Replace all em dashes with hyphens (`-`), en dashes (`–`), commas, or colons.

2. **[R-03 Mobile Responsiveness — Touch Target Deficiencies]**
   - **Location**: `index.html` lines 129–135 (`#nav-cv-btn`), lines 728–734 (filter buttons), and project links.
   - **Issue**: Key interactive controls have touch target heights under 32px (e.g. `py-1.5` and `py-2` without min-height), failing the mandatory 44px minimum mobile tap target requirement.
   - **Required Fix**: Enforce `min-height: 44px` on interactive buttons or provide adequate padding wrappers.

3. **[R-25 Color Contrast — Low Contrast Metadata & Badges]**
   - **Location**: `index.html` lines 222, 226, 230, 748, 785, 822; `style.css` lines 21, 54.
   - **Issue**: `text-slate-500` / `text-slate-400` in 11px font size across glass backgrounds and category pill badges yields a contrast ratio under 3.5:1, failing WCAG AA (4.5:1 required).
   - **Required Fix**: Increase text luminosity contrast for muted secondary metadata and ensure category badge text passes 4.5:1 on light/dark themes.

4. **[R-32 Keyboard Accessibility — Non-Semantic Clickable Containers]**
   - **Location**: `index.html` lines 1052, 1070, 1104 (`.glass-card ... cursor-pointer`).
   - **Issue**: Contact cards use `cursor-pointer` on outer `div` elements, but only inner anchors receive keyboard tab focus, producing inaccessible keyboard vs mouse behavior.
   - **Required Fix**: Move card wrapper to a semantic `<a>` link with clear `:focus-visible` styling or remove misleading cursor affordances from non-interactive containers.

### Tier 2: MEDIUM Priority (Purpose-Gate — Technique Allowed, Purpose Required)

5. **[R-07 Background — Trend-Stacking Particle Canvas]**
   - **Location**: `index.html` line 93 (`#constella-canvas`); `style.css` lines 225–233; JS lines 1448–1572.
   - **Issue**: Interactive particle constellation canvas (`Constella Infinitum`) runs continuous canvas math on mousemove without serving any hierarchy or content purpose.
   - **Required Fix**: Remove the particle canvas and replace it with a clean, performant, static architectural background.

6. **[R-01 Color & Gradients — Multi-Layer Blurred Ambient Orbs]**
   - **Location**: `index.html` lines 86–90 (`.ambient-orb`); `style.css` lines 158–222.
   - **Issue**: Four massive rotating, morphing blurred orbs (`.orb-primary`, `.orb-secondary`, `.orb-accent`, `.orb-bottom`) create chaotic multi-colored glow soup (cyan, indigo, emerald).
   - **Required Fix**: Eliminate decorative ambient orb layers to give the content clear contrast and visual quiet.

7. **[R-13 Glow — Cursor Spotlight Follower]**
   - **Location**: `index.html` line 84 (`#cursor-glow`); `style.css` lines 140–156; JS lines 1414–1446.
   - **Issue**: A 650px radial spotlight follower tracks the cursor constantly, violating the glow dose cap (maximum 1–2 focus elements).
   - **Required Fix**: Remove mouse spotlight follower aura to reduce visual clutter and unnecessary redraws.

8. **[R-10 Glassmorphism — Universal Backdrop Blur Overuse]**
   - **Location**: `style.css` lines 237–272 (`.glass-card`, `.glass-panel`).
   - **Issue**: Glassmorphism is applied to every single card, navbar, modal, skill tile, and timeline element simultaneously, exceeding the dose cap (at most 1–2 elements).
   - **Required Fix**: Restrict backdrop blur strictly to the sticky navbar and CV modal backdrop; give content cards solid, elevated architectural surfaces with crisp 1px borders.

9. **[R-14 Feature Cards — Repetitive 20-Card Bento Grid]**
   - **Location**: `index.html` lines 444–710 (`.skills-flex-grid`).
   - **Issue**: 20 identical mini-cards with identical padding, layout, and copy-paste hover scale, creating visual monotony.
   - **Required Fix**: Restructure technical stack into 4 cohesive architectural domain cards (Web Systems, Mobile Ecosystem, Backend/Database, Workflow) with contextual depth.

10. **[R-04 Icons — Inconsistent Emoji Feature Icons]**
    - **Location**: `index.html` lines 358, 372, 388, 402.
    - **Issue**: About Me skill boxes use informal standalone emojis (💻, 📱, 🗄️, ⚙️) encased in multi-colored pastel background squares.
    - **Required Fix**: Replace ad-hoc emojis with refined vector SVG domain marks or clean numbered indicators.

11. **[R-19 Animations — Gimmicky Hanging Lanyard Interaction]**
    - **Location**: `index.html` lines 297–342 (`#hanging-lanyard-assembly`); `style.css` lines 421–450; JS lines 1625–1794.
    - **Issue**: A hanging ID lanyard badge drops down with simulated physics, holographic beam sweeps, and click ripple effects that distract from professional engineering credentials.
    - **Required Fix**: Replace the lanyard gimmick with an elegant, stable portrait showcase with verified status metadata.

### Tier 3: LOW Priority (Quality Locks — Consistency & Polish)

12. **[R-16 Copywriting & Buzzwords — Over-Inflated Labels]**
    - **Location**: `index.html` comments, section tags, and copy (e.g. "Constella Infinitum Particle Canvas", "Atmospheric Multi-Layer Background System").
    - **Issue**: Over-dramatized descriptive terminology that reads like AI template prompts.
    - **Required Fix**: Adopt concise, direct engineering copy (e.g. "Technical Competencies", "System Architecture", "Background & Experience").

13. **[R-29 Color Palette — Uncontrolled Color Diversity]**
    - **Location**: `style.css` lines 24–33, 57–66; `index.html`.
    - **Issue**: Cyan, indigo, emerald, sky, purple, red, and amber are all active simultaneously across glow and badge tokens.
    - **Required Fix**: Limit active theme palette to 2 core colors (Carbon/Slate base + Precision Sky/Azure accent) with Emerald reserved purely for availability status.

14. **[R-05 Layout & Page Structure — Uniform Section Cadence]**
    - **Location**: `index.html` sections (`#about`, `#skills`, `#projects`, `#experience`, `#contact`).
    - **Issue**: Every section repeats the identical composition (centered pill badge + centered H2 + centered subtitle paragraph).
    - **Required Fix**: Introduce varied editorial rhythm (left-aligned engineering headers, split narrative hero, structured project showcases).

15. **[R-15 CTA (Call to Action) — Generic Button Text]**
    - **Location**: `index.html` lines 236, 242.
    - **Issue**: Generic buttons "Explore Portfolio" and "Get in Touch".
    - **Required Fix**: Contextualize labels to "Explore Engineering Projects" and "Contact & Collaboration".
